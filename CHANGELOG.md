# Changelog

The repo has one version, its tag. Each theme also carries its own `version` in its
`theme.json`, and its own `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 0.2.0

- `folio`, the journal reference theme for one writer: posts in a list or a grid, an about
  page, a contact page and a privacy page.
- `ledger`, the news reference theme: lead story, latest list, story grids, topic blocks,
  articles with key facts and author cards.
- `darkroom`, the Photography reference theme: a portfolio of photograph series with
  carousels, grids, masonry and a lightbox, services, prints, packages and contact.
- Sites pins this release and embeds all five themes as starters.

## 0.1.0

- `company` and `minimal` move here from the Sites repository, unchanged. Sites pins this
  release by version and SHA-256 and builds them into its binary as the starter themes.
