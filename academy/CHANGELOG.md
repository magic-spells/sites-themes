# Academy

## 0.1.0

- 32 sections: the announcement bar (still or a ticker), the header (a Classes panel of every
  subject, the portal link and the ask button), the menu drawer, the ask sheet (a bottom sheet
  on phones, a drawer from tablets), the cookie notice and the footer; the hero beside a photo
  with a dated label or over a full-width photo, subjects, the class grid, the class list
  (subject tabs), the class page, the timetable, the dates, the fees, visit options, the ask
  form, testimonials (rows or a carousel), the team grid and teacher page, story, gallery
  strip, social, FAQ, the CTA band, hours and location, contact, newsletter, page header, rich
  text, image with text, the journal list and the post.
- The timetable: the week as a table of days by morning, afternoon and evening, each session
  with its subject's swatch, subject chips that dim the rest, day tabs on phones and a key of
  short names.
- Subject covers: each subject takes a cover colour from its place in class order (blue,
  brick, burgundy, ink, light blue), on its swatch, its exercise-book cover and its class cards.
- Eight schemes (feint, white, manila, light blue, ink, blue, burgundy, brick), Libre
  Baskerville and Radio Canada, and fixtures for Larkfield Academy.
