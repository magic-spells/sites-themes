# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- The theme keeps `theme.json` and `design.json` in `settings/` and its templates and section
  groups in `data/`.
- Every section names a `kind` and a Lucide `icon`, so the Add-section picker groups it and
  draws its icon.
- The panel group is labelled Panel.
- A section whose color scheme defaulted to the first scheme now has no default and follows the
  site's base scheme; sections that default to another scheme keep it: the prints band keeps
  `band`.

## 0.1.0

- First release of Darkroom, a photography theme for one photographer.
- Twenty-six sections, fourteen templates, three groups, three color schemes (Darkroom, Gallery,
  Matte) and three suggested palettes.
- The Mara Lindqvist fixtures: six series with their galleries, home hero, prints and live
  galleries, about, prints, packages, contact and privacy pages.
- Carousels on tarot 0.1.0 with its lightbox, lazy-load and scrollbar plugins: hero slideshow,
  photo carousel, photo strip, services and testimonials, with the mobile rules (no arrows,
  strips cut at the right edge, a 1px bar, dots).
- Mobile menu on `@magic-spells/bottom-sheet` 2.0.2 and `dialog-panel` 2.0.3, the Work panel on
  `dropdown-panel` 2.1.1, the contact Subject on `select-dropdown` 0.3.0, faq rows on
  `collapsible-content` 1.2.0, all vendored.
- Series index list/grid switch, remembered per reader.
- Spectral self-hosted under the OFL (500, 600, 800 and 400 italic); Familjen Grotesk and
  Fragment Mono from Google.
