# Nomad

## 0.1.0

- 28 sections: the header, footer, announcement, mobile menu and cookie notice; the hero, route
  map, place and trip headers, places index, trip list, itinerary and stamp wall; the post list,
  post article, author card, start here and cost table; the gallery and film; page header, rich
  text, image with text, gear list, passport, faq, newsletter and contact.
- Home, journal, post, place, trip, places, trips, photos, gear, uses, about, contact and page
  templates, and the 404, password and maintenance pages.
- The Sand, Dusk and Night Train schemes, the drawn world map and the luggage tag, date stamp,
  postmark and contour motifs.
- Ilse Varga fixtures: 12 places, 4 trips, 12 stops, 14 posts, 15 costs, 17 gear rows, 6
  galleries and the contact and newsletter forms.
- Matches the final design: counts are visited only (the next stop is never counted), the route
  map draws no route line unless asked (the trip header keeps its trip's line) and centres on its
  list from 48rem, contact and password fields have visible labels, and dates print as
  "Oct 7, 2026".
- No reading times and no cover caption on posts. The journal's topics are pill tabs with the
  grid and list switch as icons at the right; gallery carousels have equal-width slides (square,
  4:3, 3:2 or natural) on tarot; the announcement scrolls as a ticker; selects are Magic Spells
  select dropdowns; the hero tag leans 3° clockwise and is never clipped; the header is the wordmark
  and the menu only (no "Now in" line; the menu icon alone on a phone); the newsletter field is
  labelled by its placeholder alone; card date stamps sit at the picture's top-left corner.
- Vendored Magic Spells components and build wiring.
