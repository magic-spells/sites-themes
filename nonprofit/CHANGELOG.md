# Nonprofit

## 0.1.0

- The first release, for a community charity: seven schemes (paper, chalk, oat, clay, moss,
  lake and night), Parkinsans and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next self-hosted, and three palettes
  (Commons, Bluebell and Paper).
- Sections: the announcement bar (a ticker with a pause button, dismissable), the header (the
  ask button, the help link, a hamburger below 80rem), the menu drawer, the cookie notice and
  the footer; the hero (an inset panel, a split band or the picture behind the type), the page
  header, rich text, image with text (one picture or three capsules), the FAQ, the newsletter
  and the contact form.
- Forms render the workspace's fields with errors tied to each field; the volunteer, contact
  and newsletter forms ship as fixtures.
- Fixtures for Wenlow Community Kitchen: programmes, events, posts, the team and the pages
  that show them.
- The lists and their pages: programmes (a grid, and each programme's page); events (cards,
  a carousel past three, or rows under each month, and each event's page); news (a feature,
  a grid or a list, with topic links, and each post's page); the team; visit us (each
  location's address and hours); reports; partners (a grid or a marquee, at a regular or a
  large logo size); and social tiles.
- The asks: the giving band (an inset panel or a band, with what a gift does), impact (dated
  figures and where a gift goes), ways to help, ways to give, the volunteer form beside its
  roles, a story (a post, or quotes taking turns) and an embed for a provider's form.
- Every scheme reads at 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for field borders under all three palettes.
  Pictures carry their asset's own alt text.
