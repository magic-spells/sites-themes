# Portfolio

## 0.1.0

- The first release: a theme for one person's work, CV and writing, on Mona Sans and five
  colour schemes (paper, white, stone, ink, cobalt), with the Gallery and Moss palettes.
- 24 sections: the header, announcement bar, mobile menu, footer and cookie notice; the hero
  (the name, a two-tone statement, or a picture with the name under it), statement, page
  header, rich text, image with text, call to action, newsletter, contact and contact band;
  collections, work index, work detail, CV, about, clients, writing list, post, gallery strip
  and gallery grid.
- Home, work, post, page and 404 templates, with About, Contact, CV, Work and Writing pages;
  the password and maintenance pages on paper.
- Category filtering on the work index through `?category=` links.
- One outlined button everywhere; text never sits on a picture.
- Matched to the final Figma pages: a lighter, more neutral paper and stone, every text link a
  1px underline, the footer status line as text, and the password page as a narrow column.
