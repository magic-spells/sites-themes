# Portfolio

## 0.2.1

- The password and maintenance pages follow the site's base scheme instead of naming the first scheme, so a change of base scheme in the Panel reaches them too.

## 0.2.0

- The panel group is labelled Panel.
- A section whose color scheme defaulted to the first scheme now has no default and follows the
  site's base scheme; sections that default to another scheme keep it: the contact band, call to
  action, newsletter and statement keep `stone`, and the announcement bar and footer `ink`.
- The starter templates, section groups and presets no longer store the first scheme, so a new
  site's sections follow its base scheme too. A stored `paper` that overrides its section's own
  default (the statement's Centred preset, and the statement on About and CV) keeps its value.

## 0.1.2

- Neutral greys in every scheme; the footer's ink is #171717.
- The hero's name layout drops its status line, and the role line sits closer to the name.

## 0.1.1

- Links, the filter rule and the status dot in the text colour by default; the accent colour is
  a design setting.

## 0.1.0

- The first release: a theme for one person's work, CV and writing, on Mona Sans and five
  colour schemes (paper, white, stone, ink, cobalt), with the Gallery and Moss palettes.
- 24 sections: the header, announcement bar, mobile menu, footer and cookie notice; the hero
  (the name, a two-tone statement, or a picture with the name under it), statement, page
  header, rich text, image with text, call to action, newsletter, contact and contact band;
  collections, work index, work detail, CV, about, clients, writing list, post, gallery strip
  and gallery grid.
- Home, work, post, page and 404 templates, with About, Contact, CV, Work and Writing pages;
  the password and maintenance pages on paper.
- Category filtering on the work index through `?category=` links.
- One outlined button everywhere; text never sits on a picture.
- Matched to the final Figma pages: a lighter, more neutral paper and stone, every text link a
  1px underline, the footer status line as text, and the password page as a narrow column.
