# Sojourn

## 0.1.0

- 32 sections: the header (transparent over the cover hero, solid on scroll, the booking pill
  and the round menu button), the ticker with pins between its phrases, the mobile menu drawer,
  the footer colophon and the cookie notice; the hero (cover with an image or a looping video,
  inset or centred, with parallax), intro, page header (alone or over a photo), image with text
  (straight or slanted edge, caption card), rich text, call to action, FAQ, policies,
  newsletter, contact, facts, location, amenities, reviews (a grid or one quote at a time),
  journal list (feature or grid), post, social and the booking band with the booking app's
  frame; and the signature sections: stays grid, stays featured, stay detail, gallery collage,
  gallery grid, gallery strip, explore grid, explore detail and story.
- Lakeside front page; stay, experience and post pages; stays, things to do, gallery, about,
  journal, contact, policies and page templates; the 404, password and maintenance pages on
  night.
- Seven colour schemes (paper, white, night, forest, lake, sunset, brick), the photo well with
  its paper price chip and caption card, and an optional dot-grid texture.
- 36 line glyphs in one sprite; 24 amenity words each show their own, anything else a tick.
- Booking through the workspace's booking app: a link from every booking button, its own frame
  on the booking band, or both, all set once in the Place settings.
- Fern Hollow fixtures: eight stays, nine things to do, eight reviews, six posts, eleven
  galleries, two forms and 34 pages.
- Headings rise in word by word and cards fade up while Reveal on scroll is on.
- Vendored Magic Spells components, Figtree (OFL) and build wiring.
